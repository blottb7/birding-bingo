Tap a bird to mark or unmark it. The center is free. Your card saves in this browser. Choosing a date and pressing “Use this date” starts a new card.

Each card draws 24 different birds, weighted by how often they appeared on complete checklists at Montrose Point in the selected date window, without replacement. Percentages are historical checklist reporting rates, not a prediction of what you will see.

Source: eBird Basic Dataset, version EBD_relAug-2022 (Montrose records from January 2012–August 2022). Recommended citation · eBird terms of use. This is an educational, noncommercial derived product and is not endorsed by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology.
