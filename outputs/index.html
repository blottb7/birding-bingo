Tap a bird to mark or unmark it. The center space is always free. Your card saves in this browser.

Each card draws 24 different birds with probability proportional to their historical checklist reporting frequency, without replacement. The sample covers complete checklists at eBird hotspot L161180 from October 3–17 across the available years. Percentages are historical frequencies, not a prediction of what you will see.
